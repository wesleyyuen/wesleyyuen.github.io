Online Leaderboard

Designed and implemented an online leaderboard using Unity's leaderboard service API, allowing users to post their game completion time and compare their time to other players online!

The leaderboard feature introduced a speedrunning and competitive aspect to the game, and has since accumulated 145+ entries in the leaderboard.
